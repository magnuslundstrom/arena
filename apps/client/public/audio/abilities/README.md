# Ability sound effects

These 12 WAV cues are the reviewed, sample-based effects currently used by Arena
Browser. They are generated deterministically by `tools/generate-ability-sfx.py`.
The source recordings are all CC0 and documented in `tools/sfx-sources/README.md`;
no audio from commercial games is included. Abilities without a reviewed cue remain
silent until their class sound set is designed.
