# Sound-design source material

The sample-based reference cues use the following Creative Commons Zero material:

- **Ice Spells** by bart, downloaded from OpenGameArt.org (`ice/coldsnap.wav`,
  `ice/ice.wav`): https://opengameart.org/content/ice-spells
- **Teleport Spell** by Ogrebane, downloaded from OpenGameArt.org
  (`teleport.wav`): https://opengameart.org/content/teleport-spell
- **Impact Sounds** by Kenney (`blade-impact`, `body-impact`, and `shield-bell`):
  https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
- **Short Wind Sound** by remaxim: https://opengameart.org/content/short-wind-sound
- **Swishes Sound Pack** by artisticdude:
  https://opengameart.org/content/swishes-sound-pack
- **Glass Break** by Till Behrend: https://opengameart.org/content/glass-break
- **Ghost Breath** by qubodup: https://opengameart.org/content/ghost-breath

All three source packs were explicitly published under CC0. The generated ability
cues combine, edit, pitch, filter, and layer these sources; they do not contain
audio extracted from World of Warcraft or another commercial game.
